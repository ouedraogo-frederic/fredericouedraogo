01 · ABOUT

Science at the interface between soils, contaminants and agroecosystems.

My research focuses on the biogeochemical behaviour and ecotoxicological implications of contaminants in agricultural environments. I combine field observations, laboratory experiments, analytical approaches and modelling to understand contaminant mobility, transformation and bioavailability.

My work connects environmental chemistry with soil science, agroecology and risk assessment, from molecular processes to the scale of agricultural systems.

02 · RESEARCH

Current research direction

Understanding the fate of organic trace contaminants in agroecosystems under organic waste recycling.

01

Organic waste recycling & contaminant risks

Agricultural recycling of organic waste can improve soil fertility while also introducing contaminants. My research aims to understand this trade-off and support safer circular-economy practices.

Organic amendmentsCompostsDigestatesCircular economy
02

Organic trace contaminants

Characterising the diversity, occurrence and environmental behaviour of pesticides, pharmaceuticals, PFAS and other organic contaminants across soils, water, waste and plants.

Biogeochemical fate

RESEARCH PROJECT · SYNTHESIS

Biogeochemical fate of organic trace contaminants in agroecosystems

The project follows the entire recycling chain: waste → treatment → organic residual products → soil → water/air → crops → environmental & health risks.

01Characterise contaminant abundance, diversity and variability
02Identify and rank the determinants of contaminant fate
03Integrate experiments, analytical chemistry and modelling
04Support risk mitigation and sustainable recycling
03 · APPROACH
01

Field

Sampling agricultural soils, waters, organic residual products and plants; documenting farming and waste-recycling practices.

02

Laboratory

Microcosms, incubations, chemical extractions and multi-matrix analysis to follow contaminant dynamics.

03

Data & modelling

Statistical analysis, molecular/environmental classification and process-based modelling to interpret contaminant behaviour.

04

Risk

Connecting occurrence and fate to environmental, ecological and human-health concerns in a One Health perspective.
